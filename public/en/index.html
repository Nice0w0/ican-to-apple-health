A free iOS Shortcut that puts glucose readings from iCan (i3, i6) and Sibionics / Sinocare continuous glucose monitors into Apple Health, with their real measurement times and no duplicates. No Android, xDrip+, Juggluco, Nightscout or Xcode.

How to use it

  1. In the iCan app, open your CGM device and tap ข้อมูลน้ำตาล (glucose data).
  2. Tap บันทึกลงในโทรศัพท์ (save to phone). You get an .xls file and the share sheet opens.
  3. In the share sheet, pick Sync iCan to Apple Health.
  4. The first time, allow access to Apple Health.
  5. Wait for it to finish. Readings appear in Health → Blood Glucose.

The first run imports the whole export, which can take a few minutes for hundreds of readings. After that only readings newer than the latest one in Health come in.

“I read that this is impossible”

The usual answer is that the iCan app has no Apple Health support and no export, so your options are typing readings into Health by hand, a bridge app, an Android phone running xDrip+ or Juggluco feeding Nightscout, or waiting for Sinocare to update the app.

The first half is still true: the iCan app has no Apple Health switch. The second half is out of date. The iCan app's share button exports an .xls file, and Apple's own Shortcuts app can write to Apple Health. The one missing piece was reading that .xls, and that is what this shortcut does.

FAQ

Does the iCan CGM app sync with Apple Health?

Not by itself: the iCan Health / iCan CGM app has no Apple Health switch, but it can export your readings as an .xls file. This shortcut reads that file and logs each reading into Apple Health with its real measurement time.

How do I get iCan readings into Apple Health on iPhone?

Install the shortcut from this page once. Then whenever you want to sync, export / share your data in the iCan app and pick Sync iCan to Apple Health in the share sheet. Readings appear in Health → Blood Glucose.

Do I have to enter readings into Apple Health by hand?

No. A CGM records a reading every three minutes, about 480 a day, far too many to type in. The shortcut logs every one with its real measurement time.

Do I need a bridge app, Android, xDrip+, Juggluco or Nightscout?

No. You need an iPhone, the iCan app and Apple's built-in Shortcuts app. No server to set up, no other app account.

Does it sync automatically in the background?

Not yet. You share the export from the iCan app yourself. The first run imports the whole file and can take a few minutes; after that only new readings come in, so frequent syncs are quick.

Does it work with iCan i3, i6 and Sibionics?

Yes, with exports from the Thai-language iCan app, in both the original format and the one introduced in its September 2026 update. Reading times are read as Thai time (UTC+7); outside that timezone, run your own copy with your own offset (see the README).

Will syncing again create duplicate readings?

No. The shortcut asks Apple Health for its newest glucose reading first and only imports readings taken after it.

Where is my glucose data stored?

Only in Apple Health on your iPhone. The export is sent to a converter that returns the readings and keeps nothing: no database, no accounts.
